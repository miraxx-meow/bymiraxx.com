Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mariah Taylor O/A By Miraxx ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you visit our website or use the Neumii: ADHD Burnout Toolkit mobile application.

🛡 Strict Privacy Promise: We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information under any circumstances. Information is collected solely to provide app functionality, process crash reports, deliver push notifications, and support user feedback.

1. Information We Collect

We keep data collection minimal and purpose-bound:

  • Direct Communications & Feedback: When you email us directly or submit feedback/bug reports, we collect your provided name, email address, and message details to help troubleshoot issues.
  • Crash Reports & Diagnostic Telemetry: Through Firebase Crashlytics, we automatically collect anonymized crash data, device state information, and stack traces when the app encounters an unexpected error. This data helps us identify and fix bugs.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Through Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), we process unique device push tokens necessary to deliver optional notifications to your device.
  • Technical & Server Logs: Our website hosting and security providers (GitHub Pages, Cloudflare) automatically collect standard technical infrastructure logs (e.g., IP address, browser type, and access timestamp) for security and operational monitoring. We do not run third-party advertising tracking scripts or marketing cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively for:

  • Providing and maintaining core app functionality across open testing and public releases.
  • Reviewing and addressing app feedback, bug reports, and feature requests to improve Neumii.
  • Detecting, investigating, and resolving app crashes and performance issues via diagnostic tools.
  • Delivering push notifications or communicating essential product updates regarding your account or app experience.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

We do not share your information with third parties for commercial purposes, except for the necessary service providers that enable app infrastructure, security, and analytics workflows:

  • Google LLC (Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Google Play Console): Used for crash diagnostics, push notification routing, and app delivery management.
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: Security, gateway protection, and network routing provider.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): Web infrastructure hosting provider for this website.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your contact communications and submitted feedback only for as long as necessary to address your inquiries and support app improvements. Diagnostic logs and crash reports are retained in accordance with standard retention cycles maintained by Firebase. Your personal data can be permanently deleted upon request.

5. Global User Rights (GDPR, PIPEDA & CCPA/CPRA)

Regardless of where you reside globally—including Canada, the European Union, the United Kingdom, or California—you hold the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You may request confirmation of whether we hold your personal information or communication history.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You have the right to request that we permanently delete your email records and feedback entries at any time.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You may opt-out of receiving future update communications or disable push notifications in your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Our website and mobile application are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain legal jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
