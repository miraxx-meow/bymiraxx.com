Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mariah Taylor O/A By Miraxx ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you visit our website or submit your email address for the closed beta testing of the NipIt mobile application.

🛡️️ Strict Privacy Promise: We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information under any circumstances. Your email is collected solely to grant you access to the NipIt closed beta and communicate essential product updates.

1. Information We Collect

We keep data collection minimal and purpose-bound:

  • Email Address: When you voluntarily submit the closed beta signup form, we collect the email address associated with your Google Play Account.
  • Technical & Server Logs: Our website hosting provider (GitHub Pages) automatically collects standard technical infrastructure logs (e.g., your IP address, browser type, and access timestamp) for security and operational monitoring. We do not run third-party tracking scripts or analytics cookies on this site.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your email address is used exclusively for:

  • Adding your Google Account to the authorized tester list in the Google Play Console for closed beta access.
  • Sending direct communications regarding your beta access, setup instructions, or vital testing announcements related to NipIt.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

We do not share your information with third parties, except for the necessary service providers that enable the closed beta testing workflow:

  • Formspree: Form processing service used to securely receive and route your email submission.
  • Google Play Console (Google LLC): Used to grant your Google account access to download the closed beta build.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): Web infrastructure hosting provider for this website.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your email address only for as long as necessary to conduct the NipIt closed beta testing phase. Your email address will be permanently deleted from our records upon the conclusion of the closed beta testing period, or sooner if you explicitly request deletion.

5. Global User Rights (GDPR, PIPEDA & CCPA/CPRA)

Regardless of where you reside globally—including Canada, the European Union, the United Kingdom, or California—you hold the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You may request confirmation of whether we hold your email address.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You have the right to request that we permanently delete your email address from our tester lists at any time. Requesting deletion will remove your access to the closed beta build.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You may opt-out of receiving future beta update communications.

6. Children's Privacy

Our website and beta program are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain legal jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
